About Tide Twist

Tide Twist: Current Flip is a hyper-casual arcade survival game. You play as a glowing surfer riding between two lanes in a narrow ocean channel. One tap flips your lane — but the tide controls how fast you can move!

A torrent of ocean debris — buoys, driftwood, and sea rocks — flows down from above. Dodge everything as long as you can. Every tenth of a second alive scores points, and threading a near-miss earns a bonus. The longer you survive, the faster and more complex the obstacle patterns become.

Controls

  • Tap / Click — flip to the other lane
  • Space or Enter — flip to the other lane (keyboard)

Tide Cycle

The tide pulses on a 6-second sine cycle, shown by the meter at the top of the screen. As the tide surges, the channel glows shift from cool blue to warm amber and your flip speed increases sharply — use that burst to dodge tricky patterns! Watch the meter and plan your flips around the tide peaks.
